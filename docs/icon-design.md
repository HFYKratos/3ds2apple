# App icon

## Concept

3ds2apple turns a dual-screen handheld into a native iPhone/iPad app. The icon
abstracts that idea into three elements:

- a wide upper screen and a smaller lower screen, stacked like the console's
  two displays;
- a single leaf growing from the upper-right corner, for the "apple" half of
  the name;
- a deep navy-to-teal background with a soft glow, so the screens read at
  small sizes.

It deliberately contains no Nintendo/Apple logos, no game characters and no
text; the screens are generic glowing surfaces rather than a specific game.

## Assets

The checked-in icon lives in `ios/app/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/`.
It is generated from a 4096 px master and downsampled to the iOS sizes declared
in `Contents.json`.
